The route-based Contact Earth page exposes a top command menu with a Location text field, Time dropdown, and Enter submit. The app advertises 69,580 populated places from the global city array. Browser verification confirmed the form computed as a three-column grid at a 1280px viewport: location field, time select, and Enter button on one horizontal row. Typing `Tokyo` and pressing Enter resolved Tokyo, Japan from the array, updated the applied state to `Centered scene · Tokyo`, showed `35.69°N · 139.69°E`, and rendered a centered marker and detail card. Changing the time dropdown to `12:00 PM` and pressing Enter again updated the command status to `Centered on Tokyo, Japan at 12:00 PM`, the detail card to `12:00 PM local`, and the scene metadata to `12:00 PM · 35.69°N · 139.69°E`.
