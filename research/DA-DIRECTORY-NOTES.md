# District-attorney directory research

## Key coverage finding

There is no single official, current, nationwide public bulk directory for every local district attorney or equivalent county prosecutor in the United States. Titles and jurisdiction structures vary by state, including district attorney, state's attorney, county prosecutor, commonwealth's attorney, prosecuting attorney, and circuit solicitor. A nationwide Wikipedia list explicitly describes itself as a dynamic list that may never satisfy completeness standards, so it is not suitable as the sole source for claiming a complete local-DA roster.

## Authoritative source used for the first directory layer

The U.S. Department of Justice U.S. Attorneys Listing is an official federal source and states that it lists current leadership for all 94 federal districts. URL: https://www.justice.gov/usao/us-attorneys-listing. The page was updated August 25, 2026. It provides the official's name and federal district jurisdiction but not a direct email or office phone for each row. The directory therefore links each record to the DOJ source and clearly labels the route as a federal prosecutor listing rather than a county district attorney.

## Local-source examples reviewed

State-level official or association directories exist, but are fragmented: Wisconsin provides a state government DA-by-county directory at https://dait.wi.gov/Pages/da-prosecutors.aspx; North Carolina provides a state district-attorney finder at https://www.ncdistrictattorney.org/citizens/find-your-da/; Pennsylvania provides an association DA directory at https://www.pdaa.org/da-directory/; Massachusetts provides a state directory of district-attorney offices at https://www.mass.gov/directory-of-district-attorney-offices; and Missouri provides a prosecutor directory at https://www.prosecutors.mo.gov/directory.

## Product decision

Add a Prosecutors contact layer for the 94 current U.S. Attorneys as a verified federal roster, with name, federal district, jurisdiction type, official DOJ source link, and DOJ main phone as the only phone that is directly published on the listing page. Do not label these records as local county district attorneys, and do not fabricate direct emails or office phone numbers. Keep the UI ready for future state-by-state local DA imports with a source and jurisdiction field for each record.
