# Battery responsive verification

The Battery workspace was captured at desktop 1280×720 and mobile 375×812. At desktop, the sidebar, breadcrumb, mode tabs, telemetry cards, photovoltaic controls, estimate, and safety notice fit the Bonds Studio layout without clipping. At mobile, the compact header and stacked cards preserve readable labels, both Battery tabs remain accessible, sliders span the available content width, and the photovoltaic metrics stack vertically without horizontal overflow.

The mobile capture also confirms the browser telemetry fallback and the explicit statement that the photovoltaic model is a simulation and cannot route photons or lux into the device charging circuit.
